# Comparative research protocol — completed wake-word series and future methods

**Research objective:** enable repeatable **objective measurements** and **subjective listening comparisons** of TECHO5's open microphone-processing path and Athena's original factory speech frontend, on the **same physical hardware**. The benchmark does not start from an assumption that either frontend must win. The adapter and native wrapper are measurement tools, not proposed permanent replacements for TECHO5.

**Record status:** the October 23 wake-word activation series below is **completed**. Additional subjective listening, command/STT and extended stability procedures are **proposed**, not results already achieved.

The labels **mono/stereo** below refer solely to **the Echo's own
built-in loudspeaker playback of background music**, not the number of
microphones or an ASP beamforming/processing mode. In every condition
the same four-microphone input array is used and the wake-word frontend
receives mono 16 kHz PCM.

| Tested configuration | Echo's background-music playback | Microphone / speech frontend |
|---|---|---|
| Stock TECHO5 + mono music | mono via built-in Echo speakers | stock TECHO5 microphone processing |
| Stock TECHO5 + stereo music | stereo via built-in Echo speakers | stock TECHO5 microphone processing |
| FrankenASP + mono music | mono via built-in Echo speakers | original Amazon voice frontend, physical AWB reference |
| FrankenASP + stereo music | stereo via built-in Echo speakers | original Amazon voice frontend, physical AWB reference |

**Scope of results published on 23 October 2026:** Only
wake-word activations with background music **actually played by the
Echo itself** were scored. Scripted voice stimuli and optional TV
interference were generated by other spatially positioned speakers.
Post-wake command capture, STT transcription and spoken-command accuracy
were **not** evaluated; they are separate planned experiments.

### Objective measurement principles

For future isolated comparisons, control the wake model, sound level,
room positions, playback content, and background; **log the configured
and observed effective thresholds** rather than assuming that they are
identical across speaker-playback states. Report detections, near misses,
misses, false positives where measured, latency and clipped onsets.
Post-wake command transcription (WER/CER) and sensitivity to source
direction, interference and speech level are **future work**, not part
of the initial results.

### Subjective evaluation (planned; not yet executed)

When comparing processed **microphone signals**, use the same underlying speech
material and listening playback chain where feasible. Randomize presentation
order and anonymize processor labels for listeners; document any level matching,
normalization, audible artifacts, intelligibility judgments and listener
agreement. Keep clean speech and spatially interfering sources as distinct
test conditions. Do **not** mistake enjoyment of music on Echo speakers for
a subjective assessment of its captured speech PCM, and do not present
unperformed listening trials as evidence of superiority.

### Reproducibility, safety and provenance

Each session records Git commit, actual runtime binary, DSP backend
**confirmed active**, MWW model and threshold, AEC reference origin,
channel mapping, whether IDME calibration applied (NOT coefficient
values), timestamps and clock basis. Include CPU, memory, PCM XRUN,
lost frames, watchdog events and long-duration playback failures.

When physical trials cannot be simultaneous, describe confounders;
do not claim that different loudness or RMS alone establishes superior
AEC. Publish aggregates and only synthetic or explicitly consented
recordings. Never share unredacted device logs, private audio, IDME,
factory coefficients or original software images.

The guarded Athena proof-of-concept passed short live startup,
worker-failure and stalled-IPC recovery tests. Controlled playback-based
**wake-word acoustic trials were completed on 23 October 2026**;
see the [initial concise results](acoustic-results-20261023.md).
These trials do not replace future synchronized four-microphone/AWB
recordings, post-wake STT comparisons, or long-duration AFE stability tests.

Follow the [Stage 3 preparation checklist](stage3-preparation-20261023.md)
before declaring any recorded clip suitable for AEC comparisons: in
particular, ALSA reference-frame delivery is not evidence of nonzero
playback-reference samples. The measured stock effective cutoff was 0.75
during music, while FrankenASP remained at 0.85. Record this explicitly
in every comparison; do not silently assume a common threshold.
